## 2. Component Architecture

### A. The "Live Peer Discovery" BottomSheet
- **Behavior:** Collapsible drawer with a `peekHeight` of `40dp`.
- **Elements:**
    - **Drag Handle:** `#888888` bar at top.
    - **Header:** "LIVE PEER DISCOVERY" (Bold, 12sp).
    - **List:** `RecyclerView` containing Peer Cards.
- **Peer Card (`peer_item.xml`):**
    - **Icons:** Material Bluetooth (`stat_sys_data_bluetooth`) or Wi-Fi (`stat_sys_phone_call`).
    - **Status:** Real-time RSSI signal strength display.
    - **Action:** Compact "FLASH SOS" button (`#D50000`).

### B. Map GIS Overlay
- **Engine:** OSMDroid (OpenStreetMap).
- **Markers:** Custom high-contrast markers with anchor-bottom centering.
- **Pulsing SOS:** A red `Polygon` circle (100m radius) appearing dynamically around distress nodes.
- **Control:** Mini Floating Action Button (FAB) at bottom-right for "Snap to Current Location".

### C. Flash SOS Alert UI (Full-Screen)
- **Background:** High-frequency pulsing between `#FF0000` (Red) and `#000000` (Black).
- **Typography:** Large, bold white text (24sp+) for Sender ID and Payload.
- **Dismissal:** Large "ACKNOWLEDGE" button to clear screen and stop audio/vibration.

## 3. Navigation & Hierarchy
- **Header:** Sticky system status bar showing mesh scanning state.
- **Primary Controls:**
    - Connectivity Toggles (Bluetooth/Wi-Fi).
    - Sandbox Mode (Debug simulation toggle).
- **Communication Bar:**
    - Voice Peer Spinner.
    - PTT (Push-to-Talk) massive button (`#37474F`, turns `#D50000` when active).

---

## 4. Accessibility & Durability Considerations
- **OLED Optimized:** Heavy use of `#000000` to maximize battery life during infrastructure failure.
- **One-Handed Use:** All critical emergency actions (SOS, PTT, FAB) are placed in the bottom "thumb zone."
- **Visual Contrast:** High-contrast text on all buttons to ensure readability in smoke or low-light conditions.
