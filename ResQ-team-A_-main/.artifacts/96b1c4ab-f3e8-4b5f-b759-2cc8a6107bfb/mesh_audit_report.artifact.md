## Technical Audit

### 1. Discovery Performance
The `IroncladDiscoveryManager` is correctly configured for sub-second detection:
- **BLE**: `ADVERTISE_MODE_LOW_LATENCY` and `SCAN_MODE_LOW_LATENCY` are active.
- **UDP**: Uses dynamic subnet math (`DhcpInfo`) instead of `255.255.255.255`, ensuring compatibility with various Android hotspot implementations.
- **Wi-Fi Direct**: Alphabetical tie-breaking prevents "BUSY" (Error 2) deadlocks when two nodes detect each other simultaneously.

### 2. Connectivity & Data Pipe
- **Bluetooth**: Solid implementation of background connection threads.
- **UDP**: Functional for broadcast.
- **Wi-Fi Direct TCP**: **Gaps identified.** While the devices form a P2P group, no TCP server/client is established to create a reliable socket pipe. It currently defaults to UDP broadcast within the formed group.

### 3. Data Integrity & Resilience
- **Deduplication**: Uses a `LinkedHashMap` LRU cache (500 entries) for fast hot-path filtering.
- **Persistence**: Packets are saved to SharedPrefs and synced to the `MeshPacketEntity` Room table, allowing for background cloud sync when backhaul is available.
- **Flood Control**: `relayMeshPacket` uses random jitter (100-500ms) to prevent packet collisions (CSMA/CD behavior).

## Recommendations & Next Steps

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Implement Wi-Fi Direct TCP Sockets**: To ensure reliable large-payload transfers (like voice fragments), we should implement a `WifiServerThread` and `WifiClientThread` that activate once `onConnected(WifiP2pInfo)` is triggered.

> [!TIP]
> **Battery Optimization**: Currently, the discovery engine runs at "Ironclad" (Aggressive) settings. Consider adding a "Power Saver" mode that scales down scan frequency when the battery drops below 20%.

> [!WARNING]
> **MAC Randomization**: BLE discovery relies on Manufacturer Data since MACs are randomized on Android 10+. Ensure all nodes are consistently broadcasting their `NodeID` in the `ScanResponseData`.
